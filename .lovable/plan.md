
# 360 Marketing — Agency Website

## Color Scheme
Using **Option 2**: Deep Blue (#0A2540) + Bright Blue (#635BFF) + White — gives a professional, trustworthy agency feel.

## Pages (7 Total)

### 1. Home Page (/)
- Sticky navbar with logo, menu links, and "Get Started" CTA; mobile hamburger menu
- Full-viewport hero with gradient background, tagline, subtitle, two CTA buttons, and scroll indicator
- Services preview: 4 cards in a grid with icons, hover lift effects
- Stats section with animated counters (50+ projects, 30+ clients, 5+ years, 10+ services)
- Featured projects: 3 cards with hover overlays
- Testimonials carousel with auto-rotate, star ratings, client photos
- Client logos section (grayscale → color on hover)
- Final CTA banner
- 4-column footer with links, contact info, social icons

### 2. Services Page (/services)
- Page header with title/subtitle
- 5 categorized sections: Digital Marketing, Content Creation, Creative Services, Web Development, Physical Marketing
- Each with service cards showing icon, name, description, pricing, and CTA button

### 3. Plans Page (/plans)
- 3 pricing cards: Personal (₹5,000/mo), Enterprise (₹50,000/mo, highlighted), Custom
- Feature comparison table
- FAQ accordion section

### 4. Portfolio Page (/portfolio)
- Filter buttons (All, Digital Marketing, Content, Web Dev, Physical Marketing)
- 9 project cards in a 3×3 grid with hover overlays and category tags
- "Load More" button
- Client logos grid

### 5. Testimonials Page (/testimonials)
- 6-9 testimonial cards with ratings, quotes, client details
- Satisfaction stats (98% satisfaction, 4.9/5 rating, 90% retention)

### 6. About Page (/about)
- Company story with alternating image/text layout
- Mission & Vision two-column section
- "Why Choose Us" — 6 reason cards with icons
- Team member cards (4-6 members)
- Core values section

### 7. Contact Page (/contact)
- Two-column layout: contact form (name, email, phone, service dropdown, message) + contact info (email, phone, address, social links, business hours)
- Form validation with toast notifications on submit
- FAQ accordion

## Global Features
- Smooth scroll behavior and back-to-top button
- Fade-in-on-scroll animations for all sections
- Card hover effects (lift + shadow)
- Mobile-first responsive design with hamburger menu
- Consistent 8px spacing system with generous whitespace
- Lucide React icons throughout
- Placeholder images via Unsplash URLs
